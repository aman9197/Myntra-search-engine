# Business & Domain Context — Myntra AI Discovery Engine

## 1. Business Problem & Objectives

Myntra is India's leading online fashion, beauty, and lifestyle destination. A key metric driving e-commerce revenue growth is the **30-Day Wishlist-to-Purchase Conversion Rate**.

### Problem Statement:
Indian fashion shoppers frequently add apparel, footwear, and accessories to their Myntra wishlist, but abandon or postpone their purchase without checking out within 30 days.

### Core Operating Principle:
*"Do not solve the problem. Build a system that discovers the problem."*

The engine does not guess solutions; instead, it systematically parses **4,604 real customer reviews** across App Store, Play Store, Reddit, and YouTube to extract empirical evidence of purchase friction, off-app research leakage, and fit uncertainty.

---

## 2. Dataset Scope & Source Coverage

- **Apple App Store Reviews:** Customer feedback on iOS app experience, sizing clarity, and seller returns.
- **Google Play Store Reviews:** Android reviews detailing price fluctuations, EORS discounts, fabric quality, and delivery experience.
- **Reddit Fashion Subreddits (r/IndianFashionAddicts, r/Myntra):** In-depth shopper discussions on fabric transparency, try-on photos, and leaving Myntra for Instagram reels.
- **YouTube Haul Comments:** Comments under Myntra haul videos where viewers describe why they saved items but refrained from checking out.

---

## 3. Key Research Capabilities

- **10 Guided Growth Research Questions:** Immediate analysis of core growth friction points.
- **1-5 Star Rating Filtering:** Granular review analysis across positive, neutral, and negative customer feedback.
- **8 Shopper Demographic Personas:** Segmenting friction across Gen Z, Working Professionals, Plus-Size Shoppers, and Family Buyers.
- **Interactive Chart.js Analytics:** Click-to-filter visual graphs for immediate evidence exploration.
